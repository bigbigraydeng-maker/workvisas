The Challenge

Labour Challenges Facing NZ Employers

Understanding the pain points in New Zealand's labour market

Critical Labour Shortages

Agriculture, forestry, meat processing, and logistics industries struggle to find enough workers, especially during peak seasons.

High Training Costs

Constant turnover means repeatedly investing in training new workers, draining resources and reducing operational efficiency.

Safety & Reliability Concerns

Finding workers who understand H&S requirements and can be trusted to work reliably in demanding conditions is challenging.

Complex Visa Compliance

Navigating immigration requirements, work permits, and legal compliance adds significant administrative burden.
