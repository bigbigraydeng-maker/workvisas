Angela Meng-Yee

Co-Founder | Cross-Border Investment Advisor

Angela Meng-Yee is a cross-border investment advisor and deal originator specialising in mergers and acquisitions, foreign direct investment (FDI), and strategic capital partnerships across the Asia Pacific region. She works with growth-stage companies, founders, family offices, and multinational investors to identify and execute international investment opportunities that unlock scalable growth.

With a career spanning London, Hong Kong, Beijing, Southeast Asia, and New Zealand, Angela operates at the intersection of global capital, emerging ventures, and international market expansion. She has led cross-border partnership initiatives and strategic investment collaborations alongside leading global real estate and advisory firms, building a strong network across Asia Pacific capital markets.

In addition to her advisory work, Angela is involved in the development of MoveHub, a cross-border workforce solutions startup focused on connecting skilled workers from China with opportunities in New Zealand. The initiative aims to address structural labour shortages in sectors such as construction, infrastructure, manufacturing, and services while creating transparent and compliant pathways for international workforce mobility.

Drawing on her experience in cross-border deal structuring and international partnerships, Angela helps align employers, training providers, immigration advisors, and investment partners to develop scalable workforce pipelines that support both economic development and responsible labour mobility.

Ray Deng

Co-Founder | AI Strategy & Cross-Border Operations

Ray Deng is an Auckland-based cross-border entrepreneur and AI strategist, focused on building AI-driven cross-border systems connecting China, New Zealand, and Australia. He is the founder of MoveHub, Compass AI, and WorkVisas.work, dedicated to creating innovative solutions in AI systems, workforce mobility, and cross-border commerce.

As a cross-border builder, Ray combines deep local expertise with a global perspective to create solutions in AI systems, workforce mobility, and cross-border commerce. He bridges strategic vision with hands-on execution, identifying market gaps and building practical systems that create measurable impact across borders.

Ray focuses on AI strategy and implementation, developing and implementing AI strategies that transform business operations. From machine learning models to natural language processing, he creates intelligent systems that enhance decision-making and automate complex workflows. He also builds bridges between Chinese and Australasian markets, identifying opportunities for trade, investment, and collaboration while navigating regulatory frameworks and cultural nuances.

In the labour mobility solutions space, Ray creates platforms that connect talent with opportunities across borders, simplifying visa processes, job matching, and workforce integration for international professionals. He works with founders, investors, and cross-border operators to turn complex ideas into executable ventures.

Our Mission

WorkVisas.work is dedicated to providing legitimate, transparent, and professional New Zealand employment opportunities for overseas workers. We partner with New Zealand Licensed Immigration Advisers and connect thoroughly vetted employers, offering end-to-end support from assessment and training to on-ground assistance. Our goal is to address New Zealand's structural labour shortages while creating life-changing overseas opportunities for workers.
